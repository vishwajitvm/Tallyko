# v2.0.0 (2026-10-24)

A rebuild of Tallyko into a production-grade, multi-tenant POS for Indian shops: from a single-counter kirana to large restaurants, cafés, cloud kitchens and retail. One codebase runs on desktop web (PWA), Android and iOS.

## Highlights

- **New app and design system** (Expo SDK 57, React Native 0.86, React 19):
  - sidebar on tablets and desktops, bottom bar on phones;
  - light and dark themes;
  - accessible controls;
  - loading, empty and error states everywhere.
- **Real GST billing engine:**
  - prices are calculated on the server;
  - inclusive or exclusive tax;
  - CGST/SGST or IGST;
  - rounding to the rupee;
  - invoice numbers per financial year;
  - Bill of Supply for composition and unregistered businesses.
- **Offline billing:** bills made offline are kept on the device and synced later without duplicates (client-generated order ids).
- **Printing:** ESC/POS receipts and kitchen tickets over Bluetooth Classic, BLE, Web Bluetooth or the system print dialog; Code128, EAN-13 and QR barcodes on bills and labels.
- **Menu from a photo:** free offline OCR (RapidOCR) with an editable review step before import.
- **Online orders:** Swiggy, Zomato, ONDC and magicpin order management (manual entry plus a signed webhook), cloud-kitchen virtual brands, your own online store and QR table ordering.

  - dated rate changes that switch automatically, for government notifications;

- **Super admin console** ([016](../docs/features/016_Super_Admin_Console.md)):
  - separate platform logins (super admin, read-only support);
  - businesses, plans, per-business feature and limit overrides;

- **Database tooling** ([19](../docs/19_Database_Migrations.md)):
  - `manage.py` with `migrate`, `rollback`, `status`, `make:migration`, `db:sync`, `db:check`, `refresh`, `fresh`, `seed`, `watch` and `create:superadmin`;
  - one migration per table (44);
  - automatic RLS for new tenant tables;
  - idempotent seeders;
  - demo logins in a development-only dropdown on the login screen.

- **Restaurant floor** ([017](../docs/features/017_Running_Bills_And_Floor.md)):
  - per-item kitchen status;
  - re-orders as new KOTs;
  - partial cancel with reason and wastage;
  - on the house;
  - several bills per table, with split, merge and transfer;
  - any waiter can serve any running table.
- **Per-channel menus:** each item can have its own price on Swiggy, Zomato, ONDC or QR, or be hidden there. Channel mark-ups round to whole rupees. A channel menu view shows what each app sells.
- **Table QR ordering:**
  - each guest gets their own bill on a shared table;
  - pay at the counter, or by UPI deep link with an "I've paid" claim that staff confirm;
  - owners choose the payment options.
- **Subscriptions & sessions** ([018](../docs/features/018_Subscriptions_Demo_And_Sessions.md)):
  - a free demo (length set by the super admin), paid, complimentary and development access;
  - a 402 lock with a renewal screen, keeping offline bills safe;
  - a daily 01:00 IST check that signs out lapsed businesses;
  - subscription offers and pricing options;
  - signed-in devices, with sign out per device or for the whole business.
- **Owner controls** ([019](../docs/features/019_Owner_Watch_No_Deletes_And_Menu_History.md)):
  - the database refuses deletes by a business;
  - an owner-only watch list of cancellations, removals, comps, discounts and menu changes by staff;
  - menu versioning with restore;
  - only Tallyko archives or deletes records, on request;
  - GST is charged only with a valid GSTIN (checksum-validated).
- **High-volume UI** ([20](../docs/20_High_Volume_UX.md)): every screen was redesigned for busy counters.
  - Shared building blocks: anchored action menus, selection bars, virtualised server-paged lists, filter bars with counts, keyboard shortcuts, undo toasts, status pills.
  - **Billing:** keyboard and barcode scanning from anywhere on the page, list mode, quick cash.
  - **Tables:** live tiles with seated time.
  - **Kitchen:** columns, bump, sound.
  - **Back office:** bulk menu edits in one request.
- **Scale fixes:**
  - Password hashing runs off the event loop, so logins no longer stall billing.
  - Rate limits now assume a whole shop shares one IP.
  - Tests use their own database.

## Security

- Tenant isolation is enforced twice: JWT-scoped queries, and PostgreSQL Row-Level Security under a restricted `tallyko_app` role on every transaction.
- Suspended or closed businesses are blocked at the session level.
- Platform tokens can't reach business data, and business tokens can't reach the platform API.
- Rate-limited auth.
- Production refuses default secrets.
- Dependencies upgraded for known CVEs (Pillow, PyJWT).

## Store compliance

- Android target SDK 36 and the 16 KB page-size check.
- In-app and web account deletion.
- Privacy policy.
- iOS privacy manifest and purpose strings.
- Unneeded permissions blocked. See `docs/18_App_Store_And_Play_Compliance.md`.

## Deployment

- Everything runs in Docker Compose: development with live reload, a production web preview, Android APK/AAB builds, and production with automatic HTTPS (Caddy).
- Only free and open-source components are used.

## Upgrading from 1.x

The database schema was rebuilt. 1.x databases are detected by `manage.py`; in development, recreate with `python manage.py fresh --seed`. The old code and migration chain are archived in `legacy/`.
